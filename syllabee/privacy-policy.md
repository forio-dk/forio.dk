## Who the app is for

Syllabee, Складарик and Слогарик are one reading app in three languages, for children under 13 who are learning to
read English (Syllabee), Ukrainian (Складарик) or Russian (Слогарик), and for the parents who choose it for them. This
policy covers all three: they collect, store and share exactly the same things, and "the app" below means any of
them.

## What the app itself collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. It stores five settings on the device (the
chosen game, whether «Слоги» blurs its pictures, the Memo card count, how «Собери слово» cuts a word, and which shapes
«Фигуры» deals) and whether the full version has been bought. None of this leaves the device, and none of it records
what a child has done.

## Advertising

The app shows a banner served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed at children, so Google applies its
protections for children: no personalised ads, no remarketing, and ad content limited to the rating suitable for all
ages (G). The app does not use the Android advertising ID; the permissions that would allow it, and those for Android's
Privacy Sandbox ad APIs, are removed from the app.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK processes the device's IP address (which can indicate
an approximate location), information about the ads shown and tapped, app and SDK performance diagnostics, and the
app set ID. This is sent to Google, encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described in Google's privacy policy
and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services":

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases

«Full version» («Повна версія», «Полная версия») is a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes the ads and
unlocks more themes - three in Syllabee, four in Складарик and Слогарик. Google Play processes the payment; the app
receives only whether the purchase was made, and never sees payment details.

## Speech

Words are spoken by the device's own text-to-speech engine. The app sends it only the word or letter to say.

## Parents

Purchases are made through the Google Play account on the device, so Google Play's purchase authentication settings
and Family Link controls apply. For any question about this policy, contact forio.dk@gmail.com.
